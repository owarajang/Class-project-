What is motorsport?

Motorsport is a form of competition that involves vehicles.

For example road cars with racing varients like Imsa or Wec or prototype racing like formula one and Wec.

Formula One

This is Formula One, the pinnacle of motor racing.

It is the most prestigous sport in racing and it has global reach.

It draws in crowds of hundreds of thousands of fans every race.

World Endurance Championship

WEC is the World endurance championship.

They go to about Six or Seven race tracks across the world and race for up to 24 hours.

IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship

The IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship is a North American sports car racing series.

The series is the American counterpart to the European Le Mans Series.
